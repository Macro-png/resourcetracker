Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

ResourceryLite is a free, offline-capable web app. This policy explains exactly what data is collected and how it is used.

Data I collect

None. ResourceryLite stores all your character data locally on your own device using your browser's localStorage. Your characters, resources, and session data never leave your device and are never sent to any server.

Analytics

This app does not use any analytics, tracking scripts, or third-party data collection tools.

Hosting & server-side analytics

This app is hosted on Cloudflare. As part of serving the app, Cloudflare may collect basic request data such as IP addresses, country of origin, and request counts. This data is processed by Cloudflare in accordance with their privacy policy. I do not have access to individual-level data.

Cookies

This app does not use cookies.

Third-party services

This app links to Ko-fi for optional voluntary support. Clicking that link takes you to Ko-fi's website, which has its own privacy policy. I do not share any data with Ko-fi.

Children's privacy

This app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will reflect that. I will never introduce data collection without updating this policy first.

Contact

Questions? Reach out via Ko-fi.
